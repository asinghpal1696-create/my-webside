National Publication

Materials Management Review (MMR)

Materials Management Review (MMR), is the Professional Journal for, of and by the Materials and Supply Chain Management Industry in India. It seeks to be the voice of the industry – and also the eyes, ears, and nose with in-depth coverage of the latest concepts, trends, interviews and opinions. MMR is arguably a very comprehensive journal in its field.

IFPSM News Letters

The International Federation of Purchasing and Supply Management is the union of more than 40 National Purchasing Associations worldwide. Within this circle, about 200,000 Purchasing Professionals can be reached. IFPMM is a non-political, independent and non-profit oriented International Organization, registered in Aarau, Switzerland.
